GFAMOUS MINERAL MARKET PRIVACY POLICY Last updated: November 12, 2026 1. Introduction GFAMOUS MINERAL MARKET is a digital marketplace designed to connect buyers and sellers of gold and other minerals. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, protect and share information when users access or use our platform. By using GFAMOUS MINERAL MARKET, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy. 2. Information We Collect Depending on how you use the platform, we may collect: - Pi Network username and authentication information. - Mineral listing information, including descriptions, photos, weight, prices and location. - Contact information voluntarily provided by users. - Messages, reports, complaints and feedback submitted to the platform. - Technical information necessary for platform operation, security and troubleshooting. Users should not submit passwords, wallet passphrases, private keys or other confidential wallet credentials. 3. How We Use Information We may use collected information to: - Provide and operate marketplace services. - Display mineral listings to potential buyers. - Connect buyers and sellers. - Authenticate users through supported Pi Network services. - Review listings, reports and complaints. - Detect and prevent fraud, scams and misleading activities. - Improve platform performance, reliability and security. - Respond to user inquiries and requests. 4. Information Sharing Information may be shared in the following circumstances: - Public listing details may be visible to other platform users. - Information may be processed by service providers necessary to operate the platform. - Information may be disclosed when required by applicable law or a lawful request. - Information may be shared when reasonably necessary to investigate fraud, protect users or maintain platform security. We do not sell personal information to advertisers. Users should understand that information they voluntarily publish in public listings may be viewed or copied by others. 5. Data Security We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, misuse, loss or disclosure. However, no online platform or electronic transmission method can guarantee absolute security. Users are responsible for protecting their own account credentials and must never share their wallet passphrase or private keys. 6. Content Moderation and Fraud Prevention GFAMOUS MINERAL MARKET may review listings, descriptions and uploaded images to maintain a safe and trustworthy marketplace. We may reject, restrict or remove content that is: - Sexually explicit, adult or otherwise inappropriate. - Unrelated to gold or other permitted minerals. - Misleading, fraudulent or suspicious. - Inaccurate or deceptive. - Harmful, abusive or unlawful. Users may report suspicious listings or inappropriate content. We may investigate reports and take appropriate action, including restricting or suspending accounts. 7. Data Retention We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for platform operations, security, dispute resolution and applicable legal obligations. When information is no longer required, we may delete it or take reasonable steps to anonymize it, subject to applicable requirements. 8. Children's Privacy GFAMOUS MINERAL MARKET is intended for individuals aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly intend to collect personal information from children under 18. If we become aware that such information has been collected, we may take appropriate steps to delete it. 9. Your Privacy Choices and Rights Depending on applicable law, users may request access to, correction of or deletion of their personal information. Users should avoid publishing sensitive personal information in public listings. Requests concerning personal information may be submitted using the contact email provided in Section 11. 10.
